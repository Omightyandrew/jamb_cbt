Built for motivated students, tutors, and tutorial centres

How candidates and educators utilize the Weekly UTME Challenge for targeted preparation.

For UTME Candidates & Students

Use the challenge as your ultimate weekly diagnostic test:

  • Track Progress: Monitor week-on-week improvement as you cover more of your official syllabus.
  • Build Exam Hall Stamina: Master the concentration needed to answer 180 questions across two demanding hours.
  • Spot Knowledge Gaps: Pinpoint exactly which subject or topic pulled down your composite score so you can revise smarter.
  • Compete Fairly: Measure yourself against high-scoring peers across Nigeria under identical conditions.

For Tutors, Schools & Tutorial Centres

Incorporate the weekly challenge into your weekend revision routine:

  • External Assessment: Direct your students to sit the weekly challenge as an objective test of what was taught during the week.
  • National Benchmarking: Observe how your class candidates rank in relation to national scores.
  • Focus Classroom Teaching: Use student performance results to decide which subjects or topics need re-teaching.
  • Important Note for Educators: ExamPilot offers this as a public, standardized candidate assessment. There are currently no teacher dashboards, class roster uploads, or private classroom tracking gradebooks.
