JAMB question-review guide

How to Use ExamPilot AI Tutor to Understand JAMB Questions

Use the Tutor to explore a confusing step, concept or answer choice—not simply to collect an answer. A useful cycle is to attempt the question, identify what is unclear, ask a focused follow-up, and check the explanation against reliable study material.

Start with your own attempt

Write down the answer you chose and the step that caused doubt. That gives your follow-up a clear purpose.

Before you ask a question

ExamPilot AI Tutor works online and requires a signed-in account. Its daily request allowance is limited, so use each question to work on a specific learning gap.

Free and Premium request limits

Free accounts can make up to 4 successful AI Tutor requests per day. Premium accounts can make up to 15 successful requests per day. The service also limits AI-generated requests to 5 per minute. This is not unlimited access; the available quota is shown in the Tutor.

Keep a question ready

When available, open the Tutor from a question in the ExamPilot answer-review flow so it can receive that question's context. You can also ask about a topic or describe the part of a concept that is confusing.

A practical question-review routine

Move from attempting the question to understanding the reasoning, then check whether you can use the idea again.

1

Attempt it first

Read the stem carefully, choose an answer if you can, and note the point where you became unsure.

2

Ask about the difficult part

Use the question-review Tutor action where available, or name the relevant topic and explain what you have tried. For example: “I used the mole ratio, but I am unsure why the coefficient is 2. Can you show that step?”

3

Request a clearer explanation

Ask one follow-up at a time: “Explain that in simpler words,” “Show a short example,” or “Which part of my method changes the answer?” The Tutor also offers simpler explanations, examples and a follow-up practice-question action.

4

Review each answer option

Ask why the correct choice fits and what makes your chosen distractor unsuitable. For a grammar or comprehension item, point to the wording you are comparing; for a calculation, ask which relationship or unit applies.

5

Check and practise again

Compare important claims with your syllabus, textbook or teacher's explanation. Then solve a fresh question without looking at the response and record any remaining gap in your personal notes.

Make follow-up questions specific

A short, focused follow-up is usually easier to learn from than repeating “I don't understand.” Try a prompt pattern like this:

“I chose [option/answer] because [my reason]. I am unsure about [step or concept]. Explain that part simply, then give one short example.”

You can also ask the Tutor to distinguish two similar ideas, explain an answer choice, or provide one follow-up practice question. Treat generated material as study support, not as an official JAMB answer key.

Use AI as support, not as the only source

Verify important explanations

AI responses can be incomplete or mistaken. Check formulas, definitions and exam requirements against reliable materials, and ask a teacher when the explanation remains unclear.

Keep a personal error log

Write down the question topic, the error you made and the corrected idea. The Tutor does not replace your own review notes or guarantee a particular score or admission outcome.

Plan around access

AI Tutor needs an internet connection and cannot answer while you are offline. If you have reached today's limit, return to your notes or syllabus and continue when requests are available again.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign in to use ExamPilot AI Tutor?

Yes. You need to sign in to an ExamPilot account before sending an AI Tutor request.

How many AI Tutor requests can I make?

Free accounts have up to 4 successful requests per day and Premium accounts up to 15. A separate rate limit allows no more than 5 AI-generated requests per minute.

Can I use AI Tutor without an internet connection?

No. AI Tutor needs an active internet connection to send a request and receive a response.

Can I rely on every AI Tutor explanation as an official answer?

No. Use it as study support and check important explanations against reliable study materials or a qualified teacher.
