Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I sign in and access my account?

Use your registered email and password on the student login page. If you are already signed in, the dashboard will load your active session. If your session is expired, you can sign back in from the Student login page.

How does ExamPilot practice work?

Open ExamPilot from the dashboard to begin the practice flow. Free users get a daily limit of 20 practice questions. Premium users have unlimited practice access.

How do I submit answers and view results?

Answer each question in the CBT flow and submit the exam or practice set. Results are shown in the results area after the attempt is completed.

Where do I find Past Questions?

Past Questions is available in the student dashboard and is premium-locked for free users. Premium users can open it to review past-year question sets with the available filters.

How do Answers & Explanations work?

Answers & Explanations is a premium feature that shows the correct option and explanation for each question when the record includes explanation data. If an explanation is not available, the page shows a clear “Explanation not available” message.

What is the Daily Challenge and how is it limited?

Daily Challenge is part of the student dashboard. Free users can complete up to 5 questions per day, while premium users get up to 15. Streak and Leaderboard are part of the same daily challenge experience.

What is the subscription process?

Use the subscription option in the dashboard to open the plan modal. Payment is processed through the project’s existing Paystack flow. Premium access unlocks the premium cards and premium features on the student dashboard.

Payment & Subscription Help

  • Free users can access the free plan features and the 5-question Daily Challenge.
  • Premium users unlock full access to premium cards and premium content.
  • Open the subscription modal from the dashboard to view the current plans and payment flow.
  • Subscription status is checked through the project’s existing Supabase subscription data and payment flow.

CBT Help

  • Use the ExamPilot card on the dashboard to begin your practice session.
  • Free users have a 20-question daily limit for practice sessions.
  • Premium users can continue without the daily practice cap.
  • Answer every question carefully before submitting the set.

Account Help

  • Update your profile from the Settings page.
  • Reset and confirm your password in the password section.
  • If you are logged out unexpectedly, sign in again from the student login page.
  • If a subscription issue persists, contact support using email or WhatsApp.
  • Review our Terms & Policies anytime.
