Organise revision into mathematical areas

Use the current official document to check the exact topics and objectives before setting your plan.

Number and numeration

Practise accurate operations with numbers, fractions, ratios, and percentages where included in your syllabus.

Algebra

Work on simplifying expressions and solving equations step by step. Check that each operation preserves equality.

Geometry and mensuration

Draw and label shapes, identify the required measurement, and select a formula that matches the shape and units.

Coordinate geometry

Relate coordinates, gradients, and line properties; keep signs and ordered pairs in the correct positions.

Trigonometry

Identify the angle and sides in a right triangle before choosing a ratio. Confirm the angle mode and units when relevant.

Statistics and probability

Read how data are presented, distinguish averages from measures of spread, and define the sample space before calculating probability.

Understand formulas instead of memorising blindly

1

Name the quantity you need

Translate the question into a target, such as a length, angle, average, or unknown variable.

2

List known values and conditions

Write down what is given and check whether a formula's assumptions fit the problem.

3

Substitute with care

Keep brackets around negative values, follow order of operations, and show intermediate steps.

4

Check the result

Estimate the answer independently and check units, signs, and whether the value is reasonable.

Original worked examples

These short examples illustrate methods; they do not define the complete syllabus.

Linear equation

Solve 2x + 3 = 11. Subtract 3 from both sides: 2x = 8. Divide by 2: x = 4. Substitution gives 2(4) + 3 = 11.

Quadratic factorisation

Solve x2 - 5x + 6 = 0. Factor: (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0, so x = 2 or x = 3. Both values make the original expression zero.

Right-triangle ratio

If the opposite and hypotenuse are 3 and 5, respectively, then sin θ = 3/5. The ratio follows from the sides relative to the stated angle.

Mean, median, and mode

For 2, 4, 4, 6, the mean is 16/4 = 4, the middle pair averages to a median of 4, and the most frequent value (mode) is 4.

Calculation practice, time management, and common errors

Practise in stages

First learn a method without a timer. Then practise several questions of the same type, mix topics, and finally use timed sets to improve pacing.

Keep an error log

Classify errors as a concept gap, formula choice, sign or arithmetic mistake, misread condition, or unit issue. Review the category, not just the corrected answer.

Use time deliberately

If a problem is taking too long, mark it for return and continue to items you can solve. Practise this approach in timed sessions rather than sacrificing several questions to one difficult calculation.

Check common pitfalls

Watch for negative signs, brackets, copied values, premature rounding, inconsistent units, and formulas applied outside their conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Which Mathematics topics should I study first?

Use a diagnostic practice set to find areas that need attention, then check every planned topic against the current official syllabus.

Should I memorise Mathematics formulas?

Learn a formula's meaning, variables, units, and conditions as well as how to recall it. Then practise selecting and applying it.

How can I reduce calculation errors?

Show intermediate steps, preserve signs and brackets, avoid rounding too early, and substitute your answer back when possible.

How should I practise under time pressure?

Build accuracy first, then use timed sets. Move past a stalled problem and return to it later if time allows.
