What is the Prescribed JAMB Recommended Novel?

Understanding how the official JAMB recommended novel fits into the examination structure is essential for every candidate.

Compulsory for Every Candidate

Every single student registering for the UTME—regardless of whether they are applying for Medicine, Engineering, Law, Accounting, Agriculture, or Education—must sit for the general JAMB Use of English paper. The official JAMB Use of English reading text forms a core component of this compulsory subject.

Direct Comprehension Questions

The examination features direct and inferential comprehension questions drawn specifically from the plot, character motives, dialogue, settings, and themes of the prescribed reading text. Candidates who read the novel thoroughly can secure vital marks in this section.

Distinct from Literature in English

Do not confuse the general reading text with elective Literature in English materials. Literature candidates study a separate, extensive syllabus of African and non-African prose, drama, and poetry. The Use of English novel is read by all 1.5+ million candidates nationwide.

Current Status of the 2027 JAMB Novel & Historical Precedents

How JAMB manages prescribed novel transitions across consecutive examination cycles.

The 2025 and 2026 Precedent

JAMB introduced The Lekki Headmaster by Kabir Alabi Garba for the 2025 UTME, replacing the long-running The Life Changer by Khadija Abubakar Jalli. The text was subsequently retained for the 2026 examination, establishing its place as the current official reading material.

Multi-Year Adoption Pattern

Historically, JAMB rarely changes the prescribed novel after only one or two cycles. Notable previous texts—such as The Life Changer, Sweet Sixteen by Bolaji Abdullahi, and In Dependence by Sarah Ladipo Manyika—were each utilized across multiple consecutive UTME sessions to ensure curricula stability.

Smart Advice for 2027 Candidates

While awaiting official confirmation in the 2027 registration brochure, beginning your study with The Lekki Headmaster is the most practical step. The literary analysis, comprehension techniques, and thematic skills developed carry directly into whichever text JAMB formally designates.

About The Lekki Headmaster and Author Kabir Alabi Garba

An insightful narrative rooted in the real-world dynamics of modern Nigerian education.

The Author: Kabir Alabi Garba

Kabir Alabi Garba is a distinguished Nigerian journalist, cultural commentator, and literary editor. Having served for many years as the Arts and Media Editor at The Guardian Nigeria, Garba brings sharp reportorial observation, social consciousness, and narrative clarity to contemporary educational issues.

Setting: The Lekki Educational Corridor

The novel is set primarily within the upscale Lekki peninsula of Lagos State. This backdrop highlights the sharp contrast between high-tuition private institutions, parental expectations of entitlement, societal pressures, and the unyielding moral responsibility required of dedicated educators.

Pedagogical Relevance

JAMB's selection of the text aligns with the Board's tradition of prescribing novels that address pressing social questions—such as youth discipline, digital distractions, ethical leadership, and academic honesty—making it engaging for teenage school leavers.

Overview, Core Themes, and Key Character Dynamics

An educational summary designed to build conceptual comprehension without reproducing copyrighted prose.

Synopsis Overview

The narrative revolves around the principled administration of a secondary school in the Lekki area. The headmaster confronts numerous structural and moral dilemmas: managing affluent parents who demand special treatment, upholding strict examination ethics, mentoring vulnerable students, and safeguarding institutional integrity amidst commercial pressures.

Theme: Integrity vs. Compromise

A central pillar of the novel is the tension between maintaining ethical standards and capitulating to financial influence. The headmaster's refusal to compromise disciplinary codes underscores the value of moral steadfastness in institutional governance.

Theme: Modern Youth & Technology

The book examines the influence of smartphones, social media validation, peer pressure, and emerging urban youth cultures. It challenges students to reflect on the balance between technological connectivity and focused academic pursuits.

Theme: Parental Responsibility

Through vivid interactions between school administrators and families, the novel explores the distinction between financial provision and active emotional parenting, demonstrating that wealth cannot substitute for deliberate moral guidance.

Character: The Principled Headmaster

Portrayed as a resolute, seasoned educator whose leadership philosophy centers on fairness, accountability, and the holistic development of every child regardless of background.

Character: The Students & Faculty

The teachers represent varied perspectives on occupational dedication in the face of economic realities, while the students mirror the aspirations, insecurities, and moral crossroads typical of contemporary Nigerian adolescents.

How to Prepare for Novel Comprehension Questions in JAMB CBT

Follow a proven four-step revision strategy to maximize your score in the Use of English paper.

1

Read for Plot and Context

Read the novel attentively from start to finish. Avoid relying solely on hearsay or superficial social media summaries. Note the chronological progression of events and pivotal turning points.

2

Map Character Motivations

Create a character relationship chart. Record each character's role, notable statements, moral choices, and relationships with other figures in the book. JAMB frequently tests who said what and why.

3

Note Literary Devices & Vocabulary

Highlight unfamiliar vocabulary, idiomatic expressions, irony, metaphors, and figures of speech used by the author. Examiners frequently test contextual word meanings drawn directly from the text.

4

Practise Timed CBT Questions

Frequently Asked Questions: JAMB Novel 2027

Quick answers to common questions asked by prospective UTME candidates.

Is The Lekki Headmaster confirmed for JAMB 2027?

As of November 26, 2026, JAMB has not yet published an official announcement for the 2027 reading text. However, The Lekki Headmaster was used in 2025 and 2026, and historical precedents indicate that JAMB typically retains novels for multiple consecutive cycles.

Who wrote The Lekki Headmaster?

The novel was authored by Kabir Alabi Garba, a veteran Nigerian arts journalist, editor, and author known for his insightful commentary on Nigerian socio-cultural and educational life.

Does every JAMB candidate need to read the novel?

Yes. Every candidate registering for the UTME must sit for Use of English. Questions from the prescribed novel form a mandatory section of this paper for all students across Science, Commercial, and Arts fields.

How is this novel different from Literature in English?

The prescribed novel is part of the compulsory Use of English paper written by all candidates. Literature in English is an optional elective subject taken primarily by Arts and Humanities students, which has its own separate syllabus of prose, drama, and poetry.

When will JAMB officially confirm the 2027 novel?

JAMB customarily confirms reading texts alongside the official release of the UTME registration guidelines, e-brochure, and syllabus updates on the IBASS portal when registration opens.

Can I score high in JAMB English without reading the novel?

Skipping the novel puts you at a severe disadvantage. The comprehension questions linked to the reading text carry valuable marks that can easily determine whether you hit a 70+ or 80+ in English Language.
