How to Get Your NIN and Generate Your JAMB Profile Code

Before you can buy a UTME or Direct Entry registration e-PIN or register at an accredited CBT centre, the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board requires every candidate to link a verified National Identification Number and generate a unique 10-digit profile code.

Essential Quick Facts

Your National Identification Number is issued once in a lifetime by the National Identity Management Commission. Your JAMB profile code is a distinct 10-digit numeric transaction code generated via SMS using that number for registration.

NIN Enrolment vs JAMB Profile Code: Understanding the Difference

Many first-time candidates confuse the National Identification Number with the JAMB profile code. They are issued by two separate statutory agencies and perform different functions.

National Identification Number (NIN)

Administered exclusively by the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC). The NIN is a permanent 11-digit national identity number tied to your biometrics (fingerprints, facial photograph, and signature). You only enroll for it once, and it serves as your official civic identity throughout Nigeria.

JAMB Profile Code

Generated exclusively by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB). The profile code is a 10-digit numeric code created when you send an SMS linking your NIN to JAMB. It is required to purchase your UTME or Direct Entry e-PIN and access your candidate profile on the JAMB e-Facility portal.

The Dedicated SIM Card Rule

JAMB enforces a strict one-candidate-per-SIM policy. The telephone number you use to send the SMS becomes permanently tied to your profile code and entire academic record for that admission year. Do not use a borrowed phone number or a SIM card previously used by another candidate.

Step 1: How to Enrol for Your NIN with NIMC

If you do not yet have a verified 11-digit NIN, complete your enrolment at least several weeks before JAMB registration opens to prevent verification delays.

Phase 1

Locate an Accredited NIMC Enrolment Centre

Visit an official NIMC State or Local Government office, or an accredited third-party enrolment partner (banks, telco service centres, or licensed private operators). You can locate verified centres directly on the official NIMC Enrolment Centres Directory.

Phase 2

Present Required Identity Documentation

Candidates must present valid supporting identity documentation. For school-age candidates, acceptable documents typically include a certified National Population Commission (NPC) birth certificate, statutory declaration of age, school identity card, or an official letter of identification from your school principal or local government council.

Phase 3

Biometric and Demographic Data Capture

The enrolment officer will capture your 10 fingerprints, facial portrait, and personal biographical details (full name, date of birth, state and LGA of origin). Ensure your name spelling and birth date match your school records and O'Level documents exactly.

Phase 4

Collect Enrolment Slip and Retrieve NIN

After capture, you receive a National Identity Enrolment Slip carrying a tracking ID. Once processing completes, your 11-digit NIN is generated and printed on your official National Identification Number Slip.

Step 2: How to Generate Your 10-Digit JAMB Profile Code via SMS

Once your NIN is active and verified, generate your profile code using standard mobile SMS messaging.

The Official SMS Command Format

Open the SMS messaging app on your mobile phone. Type the word NIN, press the spacebar once, and then type your 11-digit National Identity Number. For example: NIN 12345678901. Make sure there is only one space between "NIN" and the digits.

Official Transmission Shortcodes

Send the text message to either 55019 or 66019. Both shortcodes are officially designated by JAMB across major Nigerian telecommunication networks (MTN, Airtel, Glo, and 9mobile).

Receiving Your 10-Digit Code

Within a few minutes, you will receive an automated SMS response from JAMB stating: "Your profile code is [10-digit number], Surname, First Name...". Save this SMS carefully, write down the 10-digit number, and keep it secure.

Troubleshooting Common SMS Profile Code Errors

Candidates occasionally encounter network or formatting issues when generating their profile code. Here is how to resolve the most frequent errors.

Insufficient Airtime Balance

Sending the SMS to 55019 or 66019 incurs a standard service charge deducted by network providers (typically ₦50). Ensure your SIM card has at least ₦100 to ₦150 in regular main account airtime. Promotional airtime or bonus balances cannot be used for shortcode SMS.

"NIN Already Used" or Linked to Another Number

If you receive a notification that your NIN has already been used, it means that NIN was previously tied to another phone number. You must use the original phone number that registered the NIN or visit an accredited JAMB office to update your record.

How to Retrieve a Lost Profile Code

If you accidentally deleted your profile code SMS, send the word RESEND to 55019 or 66019 from the exact same phone number used during initial registration. The system will resend your 10-digit profile code without requiring a fresh submission.

Procedural Notice
