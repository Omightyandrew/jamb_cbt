Complete Guide to JAMB CAPS: Admission Status, Acceptance and Programme Transfer

The Central Admissions Processing System is the exclusive, automated clearinghouse for all undergraduate admissions in Nigerian tertiary institutions. Understanding the distinction between university recommendations and final JAMB approvals ensures you never miss a legitimate admission offer.

Core Golden Rule of CAPS

Any admission offered outside JAMB CAPS is invalid and illegal. Even if a university publishes your name on its internal merit list, your admission is not official until processed, approved, and accepted directly on the CAPS portal.

Institutional Recommendations vs JAMB Approval: Who Does What?

The CAPS workflow operates on a strict division of administrative authority between tertiary institutions and JAMB.

1. The Tertiary Institution's Role

Each university, polytechnic, or college of education conducts its internal post-UTME screening and determines departmental merit cut-offs based on its Senate-approved guidelines. The institution then uploads its proposed candidates to CAPS as a formal admission recommendation.

2. JAMB's Regulatory Oversight

JAMB acts as the national quality and regulatory auditor. The Board verifies that recommended candidates meet national policy benchmarks, that correct UTME and O'Level subject combinations are satisfied, and that institutional quota limits are respected. Only after this verification does JAMB grant formal approval.

3. The Candidate's Final Decision

Once approved, the power shifts to the candidate. You must log in to CAPS and formally click Accept Admission to finalize the offer or Reject Admission to remain eligible for consideration by other institutions or programmes.

Step-by-Step: How to Check Your Admission Status on CAPS

Follow these steps to access the full CAPS dashboard, especially when using a mobile smartphone.

Step 1

Log in to the Official JAMB e-Facility Portal

Navigate to the official JAMB e-Facility Portal using your registered email address and password created during profile registration.

Step 2

Enable Desktop Site View on Mobile Browsers

If accessing the portal from a smartphone (Chrome, Safari, or Opera), open your browser menu (the three vertical dots or share icon) and check Desktop Site or Request Desktop Website. Without this setting enabled, the CAPS navigation sidebar will remain hidden on mobile screens.

Step 3

Navigate to the CAPS Service Module

On your dashboard, locate and click the Check Admission Status tab. Select your examination year, enter your registration number if prompted, and click on the button labeled Access My CAPS.

Step 4

Open the Admission Status Tab

From the left-hand navigation panel on the CAPS landing page, click Admission Status to review your candidate profile, institutional choice, programme, and current evaluation status.

Understanding Your CAPS Admission Status

What do the different status messages mean during the admission processing cycle?

"Not Admitted"

Your chosen institution has not yet uploaded its recommendations, or your profile is still under evaluation. It is not an outright rejection; admission screening takes place in sequential batches throughout the admission window.

"Recommended"

The tertiary institution has screened your credentials, confirmed you satisfy their criteria, and forwarded your profile to JAMB for final statutory clearance.

"Admission in Progress" (AIP)

JAMB has acknowledged the institution's recommendation and is conducting its automated and manual verification against quota allocations and subject rules. This stage usually transitions to Approved within days or weeks.

"Congratulation, You Have Been Offered Admission!"

Both your institution and JAMB have cleared your file. Two interactive buttons will appear: Accept Admission and Reject Admission.

Programme Transfer Offers and the CAPS Marketplace

Two critical features designed to protect candidates from losing admission entirely.

Programme Transfer Consideration

If your aggregate score does not meet the cut-off for your primary course (e.g., Medicine), your institution may recommend you for an alternative related programme (e.g., Physiology or Anatomy). This appears under the Transfer Approval tab on CAPS. You must formally accept the transfer on CAPS before the university can process the admission.

The CAPS Marketplace

Institutions with unfulfilled quota allocations search the CAPS Marketplace for qualified candidates who have not yet secured admission elsewhere. If scouted, you receive an alert requesting your consent to be considered by the interested institution.

Printing Your Official Admission Letter

Once you accept an admission offer on CAPS, you can proceed to the e-Facility portal to print your official JAMB Admission Letter. This document is mandatory for physical clearance, matriculation, and National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) mobilization.

Official Advisory

Timelines, Deadlines & Institutional Policies

Admission cut-off marks and screening schedules are determined by individual institutional Senates and Academic Boards. Acceptance deadlines on CAPS are time-sensitive; unaccepted offers may be recalled and reallocated to other candidates. Always monitor your status regularly on JAMB e-Facility and cross-reference announcements on your institution's official website.
