JAMB Exam-Day Checklist: What to Bring and What to Expect

Use this checklist to prepare your travel and review your current candidate instructions. JAMB requirements can change; your own examination notification and current official notices take precedence over generic advice.

Official details to verify before exam day

Use your current JAMB candidate account and examination notification. The public official pages accessible while preparing this guide did not provide a current, complete checklist of candidate documents or centre rules, so this article does not guess those details.

Confirm your examination information

Check the date, session, venue and any instructions shown in your current official notification. If a detail is missing or inconsistent, use a current JAMB contact channel rather than an unofficial social post.

Check which documents are required

Prepare only the candidate document or documents explicitly listed in your current official instructions. This guide does not claim that a particular identification document, printout or extra item is universally required.

Verify arrival and centre procedures

Follow the arrival time and entry directions in your own notification. Do not rely on a time copied from another candidate or a previous examination year.

Check restrictions from JAMB

Review the latest official rules on items allowed at the centre. As a practical precaution—not a claim about JAMB's current item policy—do not plan to carry a phone into the examination centre. Leave any item prohibited by current instructions outside the centre.

Before leaving home

These are practical planning suggestions, not additional JAMB requirements.

1

Recheck official instructions

Read your latest examination notification once more and prepare only the listed documents and permitted items.

2

Plan the journey

Confirm the route to the centre, estimate ordinary travel time, arrange reliable transport and consider a backup route. Follow the arrival time in your notice rather than using a generic rule.

3

Make practical arrangements

Set out permitted clothing and essentials the night before, eat and hydrate as you normally would, and avoid last-minute rushing where possible.

4

Keep a trusted person informed

If helpful, tell a family member your route and expected schedule. Do not share private candidate information publicly.

At the centre

Follow staff instructions

Listen to authorized centre staff and invigilators. Ask them for clarification if you do not understand a direction; do not rely on another candidate's guess.

Use only authorized items

Keep to the current official rules and the centre's instructions. As a practical precaution, do not take a phone into the examination area, and do not bring any item identified as prohibited.

Raise a problem promptly

If your candidate details, computer or examination session appear incorrect, tell an invigilator or authorized centre staff member immediately and follow their instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Which documents must I bring to the JAMB examination?

Check the documents listed in your current official candidate notification and follow current JAMB instructions. This guide does not provide a universal document list.

What time should I arrive at the examination centre?

Use the arrival time shown in your current official notification. Do not rely on a generic time from an old notice or another candidate.

Can I take my phone into the examination centre?

As a practical precaution, do not plan to carry a phone into the examination centre. Check the latest official rules for prohibited items; this guide does not claim that JAMB currently prohibits a specific item.
