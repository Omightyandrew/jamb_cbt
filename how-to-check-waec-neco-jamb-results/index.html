How to Check WAEC, NECO and JAMB Results Online and via SMS

Each Nigerian examination body operates its own dedicated verification portal, token or e-PIN requirement, and SMS query format. Follow the exact official procedures below to check your WAEC, NECO, and JAMB UTME results securely.

Security Reminder

Only enter your examination numbers, PINs, or result tokens on official examination council websites or designated shortcodes. Avoid unverified third-party portals.

1. How to Check WAEC (WASSCE) Results

The West African Examinations Council provides both an online portal and an SMS query channel for verifying WASSCE results.

Online via WAECDirect Portal

Visit the official WAECDirect Results Portal. Enter your 10-digit WAEC Examination Number (your 7-digit centre number followed by your 3-digit candidate number), select your Examination Year and Examination Type (School or Private Candidate), and enter the 12-digit PIN and Serial Number from your Smart Identity Card or purchased e-PIN.

Official WAEC SMS Format

To check your result via SMS, compose a message using the exact format: WAEC*ExamNo*PIN*ExamYear (with no spaces between elements) and send it to the official shortcode 32327. Per WAEC’s documented SMS instructions, this service is documented for MTN, Celtel, and Glo networks. Standard network SMS charges apply.

PIN & Smart Card Requirements

School candidates typically receive a Smart Identity Card bearing their result-checking PIN, while private candidates purchase an e-PIN from authorized outlets or banks. Each PIN has a limited number of permitted result checks, so print or save a PDF copy of your result immediately upon viewing.

2. How to Check NECO (SSCE) Results

The National Examinations Council uses an online token-based verification system for SSCE Internal and SSCE External examinations.

Online via NECO Results Portal

Navigate to the official NECO Results Portal. Select your Examination Year and Examination Type (SSCE Internal or SSCE External), enter your NECO Result Token, type your Registration Number, and click Check Result.

Purchasing Your NECO Result Token

Instead of physical scratch cards, NECO requires an electronic Result Token. Candidates can purchase tokens directly through the official NECO portal using a debit card or through authorized schools and accredited vending outlets. Note that tokens have a fixed allowance of uses per candidate registration number.

Verify Details Before Submission

Double-check your Registration Number and Examination Year before clicking submit. Repeated failed entries with invalid tokens or mismatched registration details can exhaust your token checks.

3. How to Check JAMB UTME Results

The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board provides an official SMS notification service and full result slip downloads through e-Facility.

Official JAMB SMS Command

Open the SMS app on the exact mobile phone number linked to your JAMB profile during registration. Send the exact command UTMERESULT to either 55019 or 66019. Ensure your SIM card has a minimum airtime balance of ₦50 in your main account (bonus airtime cannot be used for shortcode queries).

Full Result Slip via e-Facility

Once UTME results are fully uploaded to the candidate portal, log in to the official JAMB e-Facility Portal using your email address and password to view your subject breakdown and print your official UTME Result Slip (administrative portal charges apply for printing original slips).

Use Only Your Registered SIM Card

The UTMERESULT SMS request will fail if sent from a different mobile phone number. JAMB validates the sender phone number directly against the registration profile tied to your National Identification Number.

Handling Withheld or Unavailable Results

What should candidates do if their result is temporarily unavailable or marked as withheld?

Wait for Official Board Reviews

When an examination body withholds a result or flags an examination centre for review, resolution occurs exclusively through formal administrative reviews by the respective Board or Council (such as WAEC's Nigeria Examinations Committee or JAMB's official review panels).

Beware of Fraudulent "Upgrade" Claims

No third-party agent, cyber cafe operator, or social media contact can "upgrade" scores or manually unblock withheld results. Rely solely on official updates published on jamb.gov.ng, waecdirect.org, and results.neco.gov.ng.

Always verify current portal instructions and charges directly on the official websites of WAEC, NECO, and JAMB prior to initiating transactions.
