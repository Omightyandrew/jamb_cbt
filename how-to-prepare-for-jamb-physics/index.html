Apply formulas with units and conditions

1

Translate the question

Write down the quantity required and the information supplied. Note whether the quantity is a scalar or vector.

2

Check units and assumptions

Convert to consistent SI units. Check conditions such as constant acceleration before using an equation of motion.

3

Choose and rearrange a relation

Use a formula whose variables match the known and unknown quantities. Rearrange it before substituting if that reduces errors.

4

Check dimensions and magnitude

Verify that units match the target quantity and that the result is physically plausible.

Original conceptual and numerical examples

These examples are illustrative, with their assumptions stated.

Constant-velocity concept

In an inertial frame, an object moving in a straight line at constant velocity has zero resultant force. Individual forces may still act, but they balance overall.

Final velocity under constant acceleration

A body starts at u = 3 m/s and accelerates at a = 2 m/s2 for t = 4 s. With constant acceleration, v = u + at = 3 + 2(4) = 11 m/s.

Distance under constant acceleration

For the same initial conditions, s = ut + (1/2)at2 = 3(4) + (1/2)(2)(42) = 12 + 16 = 28 m. The formula assumes constant acceleration.

Electrical power in a DC circuit

For a DC circuit with V = 12 V across a component and I = 2 A through it, P = VI = 12(2) = 24 W. The result has power units because volt-amperes equal watts.

Common calculation mistakes to check

Unit conversion

Convert before substitution; do not combine metres with centimetres or seconds with minutes without conversion.

Formula conditions

Do not use a constant-acceleration relation when the question does not satisfy its assumptions.

Dimensions and notation

Keep squared units such as m/s2 intact, distinguish symbols, and include the final unit.

Signs and arithmetic

Choose a sign convention where needed, preserve signs through substitutions, and estimate the answer to catch obvious errors.

Frequently asked questions

How should I choose a Physics formula?

Match the formula's variables and conditions to the information in the question, then rearrange and substitute consistently.

Why do SI units matter in Physics calculations?

Consistent units make formula substitutions meaningful and help expose conversion errors. Check the final unit against the quantity requested.

How can I improve at Physics calculations?

Practise one relation at a time, show steps and units, review mistakes, and then use mixed timed practice.

Which Physics topics should I study first?

Use a diagnostic to identify gaps, then check your plan against the current official syllabus rather than relying on an unverified topic ranking.
