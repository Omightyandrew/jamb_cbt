Understanding what works offline and what requires internet

We believe in absolute transparency. Here is an honest breakdown of ExamPilot's offline capabilities and requirements.

✓ Supported Completely Offline

  • Launching the cached ExamPilot study shell and CBT interface.
  • Practising with previously downloaded question sets.
  • Realistic exam countdown timers and question navigation palettes.
  • Immediate local scoring and locally stored score reports.
  • Reading previously cached literature novel chapters and summaries.
  • Re-authenticating enrolled student credentials on the same device.

✗ Requires an Active Internet Connection

  • Creating a brand-new student account or first-time registration.
  • Downloading new, refreshed, or additional question sets from the server.
  • Asking questions or getting step-by-step explanations from the AI Study Tutor.
  • Upgrading subscriptions or processing online Paystack payments.
  • Submitting official Weekly UTME Challenge competition attempts and synchronizing national leaderboard rankings.
  • Offline availability depends on your device storage and browser cache policies (e.g., iOS Safari storage limits).
