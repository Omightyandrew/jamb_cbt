JAMB Change of Course and Institution: Guidelines, Eligibility and Timelines

If your UTME score does not meet the cut-off for your initial choice, or if you discovered a subject combination discrepancy, applying for a JAMB Change of Course and Institution allows you to realign your preferences to maximize your admission chances.

Mandatory Rule

Always consult the official JAMB Integrated Brochure and Syllabus System (IBASS) before initiating any change. Never switch to a programme without confirming that your UTME subjects and O'Level credits meet the specific department's requirements.

When Should You Apply for a Change of Course or Institution?

A change of data is an intentional strategic adjustment that should only be carried out under specific circumstances.

UTME Score Realities

If your UTME score falls below the competitive departmental cut-off for your initial course (e.g., scoring 220 when your chosen university requires 280 for Medicine), switching to a related science discipline with an attainable cut-off keeps your admission prospects alive.

Subject Combination Correction

If you mistakenly registered an invalid UTME subject combination for your desired course, you will face automatic disqualification during university screening. A change of course to a discipline aligned with the four subjects you actually sat for resolves this mismatch.

Catchment and Quota Advantages

Changing to a state or federal institution where your state of origin falls under the educational catchment area can significantly improve your competitiveness under national admission quota guidelines.

The Mandatory Pre-Change Step: Check the JAMB IBASS Brochure

Before spending money on a change transaction, verify eligibility on official portals.

Consult the IBASS System

Visit the official JAMB IBASS Portal and review the e-brochure for your destination university, polytechnic, or college. Note the exact required O'Level subjects, special waivers, and compulsory UTME subject combinations.

Cross-Check on ExamPilot

Compare requirements with the ExamPilot curated JAMB Subject Combinations Guide to ensure your O'Level credits and UTME papers match faculty prerequisites across Nigerian universities.

Confirm Institution Screening Status

Check whether your destination institution has already closed its Post-UTME screening portal. Changing to an institution that has concluded its screening process will leave your application unprocessed.

Step-by-Step: How to Process a Change of Course and Institution

Follow these official steps to execute your data change correctly.

Step 1

Log In to Your JAMB e-Facility Profile

Visit the JAMB e-Facility Portal using your registered email and password. On the left sidebar menu, locate and click on Correction of Data, then select Course/Institution.

Step 2

Generate Transaction Invoice (Remita)

The portal generates a Transaction ID and Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR) for the official administrative fee (typically ₦2,500, subject to standard banking gateway charges). You can complete payment online via debit card or print the invoice and pay at a participating commercial bank.

Step 3

Visit an Accredited JAMB CBT Centre for Biometrics

Crucial requirement: Changes cannot be submitted from home or through unaccredited computer cafes. You must physically visit an accredited JAMB CBT centre or JAMB State Office where your identity is authenticated via biometric fingerprint capture.

Step 4

Select New Preferences and Print Confirmation Slip

The accredited operator will update your preferred 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th choice institutions and programmes. Review the confirmation screen carefully before final submission, and collect your newly printed official JAMB Change of Course/Data Slip.

Portal Synchronization: Why Your University Portal May Not Update Instantly

Understanding data synchronization prevents unnecessary panic after completing your change.

Database Synchronization Lag

While changes reflect instantly on your JAMB e-Facility and CAPS profile, tertiary institution portals pull candidate lists periodically in discrete batches. It often takes between 24 hours and a few days for the university's Post-UTME screening portal to recognize your updated registration details.

Permitted Change Limits

JAMB generally permits candidates to modify their choices multiple times (up to the maximum policy limit established for that academic session). However, each change incurs a separate administrative fee, and frequent alterations risk missing institutional screening deadlines.

Keep Both Slips Secure

Retain your original registration slip alongside your updated change of course slip. Tertiary institutions routinely require both documents during physical screening and matriculation clearance.

Regulatory Advisory

Official Fees and Closing Windows

Administrative fees and deadline windows for data correction are set annually by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board. Ensure all changes are submitted before the official close of the admission cycle. Always confirm current fees and accredited centres directly on the official JAMB Portal.
