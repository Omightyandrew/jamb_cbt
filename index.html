Meet the Founder

Built from personal experience. Driven by a bigger vision.

ExamPilot started with a personal experience.

Having experienced the challenges that come with preparing for important examinations, I understood that students need more than just textbooks and endless practice questions. They need better ways to practise, understand difficult topics, identify their weaknesses and build confidence before the real examination.

That experience inspired me to create ExamPilot.

I built ExamPilot independently, with a vision of making exam preparation smarter, more accessible and more effective for students. What began as an idea has grown into a platform designed to help students practise, learn and prepare with greater confidence.

My goal is simple: to make quality exam preparation more accessible and give students better tools to reach their academic goals.

I believe every student deserves the opportunity to prepare well and perform confidently.

Prepare smarter. Know more. Be ready.

Igbawua Andrew Aondofa Founder & Creator of ExamPilot
