The 40-Second Rule

180 questions in 120 minutes gives an average of 40 seconds per question. Learn the 3-pass technique to bank easy marks immediately and leave time for complex calculations.

The Mathematics of CBT Time Management

Understanding how minutes distribute across your examination changes how you approach every single screen.

English Language: 60 Questions (35 Minutes)

Comprehension passages require careful skimming, but vocabulary, antonyms, synonyms, and oral forms can be answered in 15–20 seconds each. Finish English briskly to bank spare minutes for science and math subjects.

Elective Subjects: 40 Questions Each (25 Minutes per Subject)

For your three chosen electives (e.g., Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, Government), budget approximately 25 minutes each (75 minutes total). That leaves a 10-minute emergency buffer at the end.

The 10-Minute Buffer

Never plan to finish on the final second. Use the last 10 minutes to review flagged questions, ensure no item was left unattempted, and verify that all answer radios are cleanly registered.

The 3-Pass Exam Technique: Never Get Stuck on Question 5

The single most common mistake candidates make is spending 4 minutes wrestling with a difficult calculation early in the exam.

Pass 1

The Instant Certainty Pass (Minutes 0–60)

Cycle through all four subjects answering ONLY questions where the correct answer is immediately obvious (definitions, basic facts, simple recall). If a question requires extensive calculation or causes hesitation, skip it immediately.

Pass 2

The Calculation & Deduction Pass (Minutes 60–105)

Return to skipped questions that require 1–2 minutes of calculation, formula application, or reading longer comprehension passages. Because you have already banked 80+ secure marks, your mind will be calm and focused.

Pass 3

The Review & Elimination Pass (Minutes 105–120)

Tackle the remaining difficult or unfamiliar questions. Use the process of elimination: cross off obviously wrong options. Remember that in JAMB, there is no negative marking, so never leave a question blank.

Master 8-Key Keyboard Navigation

Using a computer mouse with a stiff cable or dirty sensor wastes 2 to 3 seconds per question. Over 180 questions, that wastes up to 9 minutes!

Keys A, B, C, D

Select your chosen option directly using keys A, B, C, or D on the keyboard. This registers immediately without moving your hand to the mouse.

Keys N and P

Press N to jump immediately to the Next question. Press P to navigate back to the Previous question.

Keys S and R

Press S only when you are completely finished and ready to Submit. In JAMB CBT centres, pressing R returns to the examination screen if prompted by a confirmation dialog.
