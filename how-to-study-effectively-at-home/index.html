Tackling the Realities of Studying at Home in Nigeria

Acknowledging domestic obstacles is the first step toward overcoming them with practical solutions.

Negotiate Study Hours With Family

Unscheduled errands derail study sessions. Agree on designated quiet study blocks with your parents or guardians (such as 5:00 AM–7:30 AM or 8:00 PM–10:30 PM) so family members know not to interrupt during those windows.

Carve Out a Dedicated Study Corner

Never study in bed where your brain associates the posture with sleep. Even a small plastic desk and chair in a quiet corner of your room creates a mental trigger for focused work.

Put Smartphone Distractions on Lockdown

Social media and group chats are productivity killers. Put your phone in "Do Not Disturb" mode or leave it in another room while reading physical textbooks.

The Adapted Pomodoro Method for Exam Revision

Long 4-hour unbroken study sessions cause mental fatigue and plummeting retention. Use structured sprints instead.

Step 1

The 50-Minute Deep Work Sprint

Pick one specific topic from your JAMB Syllabus. Set a timer for 50 minutes. Read actively, summarize diagrams, or work through 15 calculation problems without switching tasks.

Step 2

The 10-Minute Active Break

When the timer rings, stand up immediately. Drink a glass of water, stretch, or walk outside. Avoid opening social media feeds during this break, as they cause cognitive residue.

Step 3

The Immediate Recall Drill

Return to your desk. Spend 5 minutes reciting or writing down the core formulas or definitions you learned during the previous sprint before opening the textbook again.

Step 4

The Long Rest After 3 Sprints

After completing three 50-minute blocks (150 minutes of deep study), take a complete 30- to 45-minute meal or rest break to allow your brain to consolidate memories.
