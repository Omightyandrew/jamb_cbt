Subject Overview

JAMB Commerce is a key requirement for candidates applying to Business Administration, Marketing, Purchasing and Supply, Entrepreneurship, Human Resource Management, and Commercial Education. The 40 exam questions test concepts of trade distribution channels, banking facilities, insurance principles, capital market operations, and international commercial documents.

Key Topics for JAMB Commerce

These core topic clusters feature regularly across UTME past questions and curriculum guidelines.

Aids to Trade & Their Roles

Banking, insurance, warehousing, transportation, communication, and advertising functions in commerce.

Home & International Trade

Wholesale, retail, channels of distribution, export/import procedures, tariffs, and trade balances.

Forms of Business Organizations

Sole proprietorship, partnerships, private/public limited liability companies, and cooperatives.

Insurance Principles & Policies

Insurable interest, indemnity, utmost good faith (uberrimae fidei), subrogation, and contribution.

Capital & Money Markets

Functions of the Nigerian Stock Exchange (NGX), Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), shares, and debentures.

Sample JAMB Commerce Questions & Explanations

Review these verified practice questions with step-by-step reasoning to understand how questions are framed in the UTME.

Question 1 · Aids to Trade (Warehousing)

Which aid to trade primarily bridges the time gap between the production of seasonal goods and their final consumption?

A. Transportation
B. Insurance
C. Warehousing
D. Advertising
Correct Answer: C — Warehousing provides storage facilities, preserving goods until they are demanded by consumers and stabilizing market prices over time.
Question 2 · Insurance Principles (Indemnity)

The insurance principle that ensures the insured is restored to their exact financial position immediately prior to a loss without making a profit is:

A. Insurable interest
B. Indemnity
C. Subrogation
D. Proximate cause
Correct Answer: B — Indemnity prevents an insured person from profiting from insurance claims; they are merely compensated for the actual financial loss incurred.
Question 3 · Foreign Trade Documents

Which shipping document serves as a receipt for goods loaded on board a ship, a contract of carriage, and a document of title to the goods?

A. Consular invoice
B. Bill of Lading
C. Certificate of origin
D. Indent
Correct Answer: B — A Bill of Lading is issued by the carrier acknowledging receipt of cargo, detailing transport terms, and serving as a transferable document of title.
Question 4 · Business Organizations (Companies)

Under Nigerian company law (CAMA), what is the minimum number of persons required to incorporate a private limited liability company?

A. One
B. Two
C. Seven
D. Twenty
Correct Answer: A — Under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020), a single person can incorporate and hold shares in a private company in Nigeria.
Question 5 · Capital Markets

The apex statutory regulatory authority overseeing the Nigerian capital market and protecting investor interests is the:

A. Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)
B. Nigerian Exchange Group (NGX)
C. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
D. Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC)
Correct Answer: C — The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is the statutory apex regulator of capital market operators, stock exchanges, and investment securities.
