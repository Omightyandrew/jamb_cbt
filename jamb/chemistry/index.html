Subject Overview

JAMB Chemistry is required for students pursuing Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Biochemistry, Chemical Engineering, Microbiology, and Agricultural Sciences. Sample JAMB Chemistry Questions & Explanations

Review these verified practice questions with step-by-step reasoning to understand how questions are framed in the UTME.

Question 1 · Stoichiometry (The Mole Concept)

Calculate the number of moles present in 44 g of Carbon Dioxide gas (CO₂). [Relative atomic masses: C = 12, O = 16].

A. 0.5 mol
B. 1.0 mol
C. 1.5 mol
D. 2.0 mol
Correct Answer: B — Molar mass of CO₂ = 12 + (16 × 2) = 12 + 32 = 44 g/mol. Number of moles = mass / molar mass = 44 g / 44 g/mol = 1.0 mol.
Question 2 · Acids & Bases (pH Calculation)

What is the pH of a 0.001 mol/dm³ aqueous solution of Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Correct Answer: C — HCl is a strong monoprotic acid that fully dissociates: [H⁺] = 0.001 M = 10⁻³ M. pH = -log₁₀[H⁺] = -log₁₀(10⁻³) = 3.
Question 3 · Electrochemistry (Oxidation Numbers)

Determine the oxidation state of Chromium in the dichromate ion, Cr₂O₇²⁻.

A. +3
B. +5
C. +6
D. +7
Correct Answer: C — Let the oxidation state of Cr be x. Oxygen is -2. Thus 2x + 7(-2) = -2 => 2x - 14 = -2 => 2x = 12 => x = +6.
Question 4 · Organic Chemistry (Isomerism)

What is the IUPAC name for CH₃-CH(CH₃)-CH₂-CH₃?

A. 2-methylpropane
B. 2-methylbutane
C. 3-methylbutane
D. n-pentane
Correct Answer: B — The longest carbon chain has 4 carbons (butane). Numbering from the end closest to the branch gives a methyl group at carbon 2, so the name is 2-methylbutane.
Question 5 · Gas Laws

A gas occupies 600 cm³ at a pressure of 100 kPa. If the pressure is increased to 150 kPa at constant temperature, what is the new volume?

A. 300 cm³
B. 400 cm³
C. 500 cm³
D. 900 cm³
Correct Answer: B — Applying Boyle's Law (P₁V₁ = P₂V₂): V₂ = (P₁V₁) / P₂ = (100 kPa × 600 cm³) / 150 kPa = 60,000 / 150 = 400 cm³.
