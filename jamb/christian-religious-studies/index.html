Subject Overview

JAMB CRS is taken by candidates seeking admission to faculties of Arts, Law, Religious and Cultural Studies, Education, and Humanities. The 40 questions evaluate textual knowledge and practical applications of biblical events, covenants, Old Testament leadership, the synoptic gospels, Jesus' parables, and early Church fellowship.

Key Topics for JAMB Christian Religious Studies

These core topic clusters feature regularly across UTME past questions and curriculum guidelines.

Creation, Fall & Covenant Foundations

Genesis creation accounts, the Fall of man, the call of Abraham, and God's covenant with Israel.

Leadership & Early Monarchies

Moses and the Exodus, Joshua's conquests, Samuel, Saul's disobedience, and David's reign.

The Prophets & Social Justice

Amos on greed and bribery, Hosea on God's unceasing love, and Jeremiah on the New Covenant.

Life, Ministry & Parables of Jesus

Baptism, temptations, the Sermon on the Mount, miracles, the Good Samaritan, and the Prodigal Son.

Passion, Resurrection & Early Church

The Last Supper, trials, crucifixion, resurrection, Pentecost, and Paul's missionary journeys.

Sample JAMB Christian Religious Studies Questions & Explanations

Review these verified practice questions with step-by-step reasoning to understand how questions are framed in the UTME.

Question 1 · Old Testament (The Abrahamic Covenant)

What physical sign was instituted by God as the perpetual token of the covenant made with Abraham and his descendants?

A. Sacrifice of a ram
B. Building an altar of stone
C. Circumcision of every male child
D. Observance of the Sabbath
Correct Answer: C — In Genesis 17:10-14, God commanded Abraham that every male child should be circumcised on the eighth day as the physical sign of the covenant.
Question 2 · The Prophets (Social Justice)

Which Old Testament prophet vigorously condemned the wealthy elite of Israel for trampling on the poor and selling the needy for a pair of sandals?

A. Hosea
B. Amos
C. Isaiah
D. Jonah
Correct Answer: B — Amos (Amos 2:6, 5:24) addressed societal injustice and economic exploitation in the Northern Kingdom, famously demanding that justice roll down like waters.
Question 3 · Gospels (Parables of Jesus)

In the Gospel of Luke, Jesus narrated the Parable of the Good Samaritan primarily to answer which question posed by a lawyer?

A. "How can a rich man enter the Kingdom of God?"
B. "And who is my neighbour?"
C. "By what authority do you do these things?"
D. "Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar?"
Correct Answer: B — In Luke 10:25-37, a lawyer sought to justify himself by asking "And who is my neighbour?", prompting Jesus to narrate the Good Samaritan.
Question 4 · Early Church (The Great Commission)

Which disciple was elected by lot to replace Judas Iscariot among the twelve Apostles in the Book of Acts?

A. Barnabas
B. Stephen
C. Matthias
D. Timothy
Correct Answer: C — In Acts 1:21-26, after casting lots and praying, Matthias was numbered with the eleven apostles to succeed Judas.
Question 5 · Pauline Epistles (Christian Virtues)

In Paul's letter to the Corinthians (1 Corinthians 13), which virtue is declared to be the greatest among Faith, Hope, and Love?

A. Faith
B. Hope
C. Love (Charity)
D. Knowledge
Correct Answer: C — Paul concludes 1 Corinthians 13:13 with: "And now abide faith, hope, love, these three; but the greatest of these is love."
