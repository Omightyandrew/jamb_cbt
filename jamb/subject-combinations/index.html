JAMB Subject Combinations

A UTME subject combination is the set of examination subjects considered for an intended course or admission path. The relevant subjects are not necessarily the same for every course or institution, so students should research their intended path and confirm the current requirement before making a choice.

Start with your intended course

Use the brochure carefully

Brochure and admission resources can help you investigate course and institution information. Use them as a planning reference, do not reproduce their content, and verify current details against the appropriate official source before relying on them.
