Subject Overview

JAMB Physics is compulsory for candidates aspiring to study Engineering, Medicine, Computer Science, Architecture, Physics, and related disciplines. The exam consists of 40 questions split between conceptual understanding and quantitative calculation. Success requires strong grounding in SI units, formula derivations, circuit diagrams, and graphical analysis.

Key Topics for JAMB Physics

These core topic clusters feature regularly across UTME past questions and curriculum guidelines.

Linear Motion & Projectiles

Equations of uniformly accelerated motion, Newton's laws of motion, momentum, and projectile trajectories.

Work, Energy & Power

Conservation of mechanical energy, elastic collisions, and power equations.

Thermal Energy & Gas Laws

Specific heat capacity, latent heat, Boyle's and Charles's laws, and thermal expansion.

Electric Circuits & Ohm's Law

Series and parallel resistors, Kirchhoff's laws, electrical energy, power, and internal resistance.

Waves, Light & Optics

Reflection, refraction, lens formula, Snell's law, electromagnetic spectrum, and sound resonance.

Sample JAMB Physics Questions & Explanations

Review these verified practice questions with step-by-step reasoning to understand how questions are framed in the UTME.

Question 1 · Mechanics (Linear Motion)

A vehicle starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2.5 m/s² for 8 seconds. Calculate the total distance covered.

A. 40 m
B. 60 m
C. 80 m
D. 100 m
Correct Answer: C — Using the equation of motion s = ut + ½at²: initial velocity u = 0, a = 2.5 m/s², t = 8 s. s = 0 + ½(2.5)(8²) = ½(2.5)(64) = 80 m.
Question 2 · Electricity (Parallel Resistors)

Three identical resistors of resistance 6 Ω each are connected in parallel. What is their effective total resistance?

A. 2 Ω
B. 3 Ω
C. 6 Ω
D. 18 Ω
Correct Answer: A — For n identical resistors of resistance R connected in parallel, effective resistance R_eff = R / n. Here R_eff = 6 / 3 = 2 Ω.
Question 3 · Optics (Refraction & Critical Angle)

What is the speed of light in a glass prism of refractive index 1.5, given that the speed of light in air is 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s?

A. 1.5 × 10⁸ m/s
B. 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s
C. 2.5 × 10⁸ m/s
D. 4.5 × 10⁸ m/s
Correct Answer: B — Refractive index n = c / v, where c is light speed in air and v is speed in medium. Thus v = c / n = (3.0 × 10⁸) / 1.5 = 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s.
Question 4 · Thermal Physics (Specific Heat)

Calculate the quantity of heat required to raise the temperature of 2 kg of copper from 30°C to 70°C. (Specific heat capacity of copper = 400 J/kg·K).

A. 16,000 J
B. 24,000 J
C. 32,000 J
D. 56,000 J
Correct Answer: C — Q = mcΔθ = 2 kg × 400 J/kg·K × (70 - 30) K = 2 × 400 × 40 = 32,000 J.
Question 5 · Modern Physics (Radioactive Half-life)

A radioactive sample has a half-life of 15 days. If the initial mass is 64 g, what mass of the sample remains after 45 days?

A. 4 g
B. 8 g
C. 16 g
D. 32 g
Correct Answer: B — Number of half-lives n = 45 / 15 = 3. Remaining mass = Initial mass / 2ⁿ = 64 / 2³ = 64 / 8 = 8 g.
