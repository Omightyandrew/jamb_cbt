JAMB Syllabus

A syllabus is a preparation guide: it helps you identify the subjects and topic areas that belong in your revision plan. ExamPilot presents syllabus-related study content for preparation and does not replace official JAMB guidance.

Subjects and topics

ExamPilot's syllabus structure groups topic areas within subjects and sections. This makes it easier to choose one area to learn, find related questions and return to a specific part of your preparation plan.

Make each study session specific

Instead of treating a subject as one large task, select a section or topic, study the underlying ideas, and use practice to check what you understand.
