Quick Admission Guide

General baseline standards established across Nigerian tertiary institutions.

100 Level Degree

Generally requires at least five relevant O'level credits (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB) at not more than two sittings, including English Language; Mathematics is required for most science, social science, and commercial programmes.

Direct Entry (DE)

Admission directly into 200 level requires accepted qualifications such as A-Levels, IJMB, JUPEB, ND, or NCE. Exact accepted grades and grade points vary significantly across universities.

Institution Matters

The same degree programme can have different subject combinations or special waivers across different universities. Always cross-check the institution-specific profile in IBASS.

Frequently Asked Questions About the JAMB Brochure

Common questions answered to help you navigate course selection, subject combinations, and admission requirements.

What is the JAMB brochure?

The JAMB brochure is the official comprehensive directory published by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB). It lists all accredited tertiary institutions in Nigeria, the courses they offer, the compulsory UTME subjects required for each course, and the necessary O'level or Direct Entry qualifications.

What information can I find in the JAMB brochure?

The brochure provides critical details including accredited universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education; list of faculties and departments; 4-subject UTME requirements; O'level credit requirements (subjects and maximum sittings); Direct Entry qualification standards; and institution-specific special consideration remarks or waivers.

How do I choose the right UTME subjects for a course?

Every UTME candidate sits for Use of English alongside 3 specific subjects related to their intended course. You must look up the exact programme in the brochure and select the three subjects designated for that course. For instance, Engineering typically requires Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, whereas Law requires Literature in English, Government or History, and another arts or social science subject.

Can admission requirements differ between institutions?

Yes. While JAMB sets general baseline standards, individual institutions frequently have unique requirements or waivers published in the brochure's "Special Considerations" section. For example, some universities may accept Agricultural Science in place of Biology for certain science programmes, while others do not.

Can I practice JAMB questions after checking my course requirements?

Yes. Once you have identified your 4 required examination subjects from the brochure, you should review each subject's official syllabus and begin practising with authentic past questions and timed CBT simulations on ExamPilot to build speed and accuracy.

Important: This guide is designed for study and planning. It is not an official replacement for JAMB's IBASS e-Brochure system. Always verify your exact institution, programme, O'level requirements, and UTME combinations in IBASS before completing your JAMB registration or applying for a change of course.
