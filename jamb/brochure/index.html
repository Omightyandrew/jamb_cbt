JAMB Brochure

The JAMB brochure is generally used as an admission-planning reference for courses, institutions and subject requirements. ExamPilot provides preparation support around these decisions; it is not JAMB and does not replace the current official brochure or eligibility guidance.

Understand course information

Use brochure information to learn which programme and institution details you need to investigate before making choices.

Check requirements carefully

Requirements may depend on the institution, programme and admission session, so confirm current details from an official source.

Keep your plan realistic

Compare your intended choices with the subjects and topics you need to study, without treating a general guide as a final decision.

What students can use it for

A brochure can support early preparation planning, but admission information can change. Use it as a starting point and verify every important detail before registration or a course decision.
