Subject Overview

JAMB Mathematics is a prerequisite for candidates applying to Engineering, Computing, Physical Sciences, Medical Sciences, Economics, Accounting, and Architecture. The paper comprises 40 questions testing logical problem-solving, arithmetic speed, algebraic manipulation, spatial reasoning, and introductory calculus. Time management and familiarity with standard formulas are essential for achieving scores above 70%.

Key Topics for JAMB Mathematics

These core topic clusters feature regularly across UTME past questions and curriculum guidelines.

Algebra & Quadratic Equations

Factorization, roots of quadratic equations, simultaneous linear and quadratic systems, and indices/logarithms.

Calculus (Differentiation & Integration)

Limits, rate of change, maxima and minima problems, and definite/indefinite integrals.

Coordinate Geometry & Trigonometry

Straight line equations, gradients, angle ratios (sin, cos, tan), and trigonometric identities.

Matrices & Determinants

Matrix multiplication, 2x2 and 3x3 determinants, and solving linear systems.

Statistics & Probability

Mean, median, variance, standard deviation, and permutations and combinations.

Sample JAMB Mathematics Questions & Explanations

Review these verified practice questions with step-by-step reasoning to understand how questions are framed in the UTME.

Question 1 · Number Bases

Convert the binary number 11011₂ to base 10 (decimal).

A. 25
B. 27
C. 29
D. 31
Correct Answer: B — 11011₂ = (1 × 2⁴) + (1 × 2³) + (0 × 2²) + (1 × 2¹) + (1 × 2⁰) = 16 + 8 + 0 + 2 + 1 = 27.
Question 2 · Quadratic Equations (Roots)

If α and β are the roots of the equation 2x² - 6x + 3 = 0, find the value of α + β.

A. -3
B. 1.5
C. 3
D. 6
Correct Answer: C — For any quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0, the sum of roots α + β = -b / a. Here, a = 2 and b = -6, so α + β = -(-6) / 2 = 6 / 2 = 3.
Question 3 · Differentiation

Find the derivative dy/dx of the curve y = 4x³ - 5x² + 7x - 9 at x = 1.

A. 6
B. 9
C. 11
D. 14
Correct Answer: B — dy/dx = 12x² - 10x + 7. Substituting x = 1 gives 12(1)² - 10(1) + 7 = 12 - 10 + 7 = 9.
Question 4 · Trigonometry

If tan θ = 3/4 and θ is an acute angle, find the value of sin θ + cos θ.

A. 5/7
B. 7/5
C. 1/5
D. 12/25
Correct Answer: B — In a right-angled triangle with opposite = 3 and adjacent = 4, the hypotenuse = √(3² + 4²) = 5. Therefore, sin θ = 3/5 and cos θ = 4/5. Adding them: 3/5 + 4/5 = 7/5.
Question 5 · Probability

A bag contains 5 red balls, 4 blue balls, and 3 green balls. If a ball is picked at random, what is the probability that it is NOT red?

A. 5/12
B. 7/12
C. 1/3
D. 1/4
Correct Answer: B — Total balls = 5 + 4 + 3 = 12. Non-red balls = 4 (blue) + 3 (green) = 7. Probability = 7 / 12.
