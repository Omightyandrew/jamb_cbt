How to Print Official JAMB Admission Letters on e-Facility

Once your admission has been processed and confirmed by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board, printing your official JAMB Admission Letter is an essential step for university registration, faculty clearance, and future National Youth Service Corps mobilization.

Essential Overview

Official JAMB Admission Letters are generated exclusively through your personal e-Facility account. Always verify that your admission status is confirmed and check the exact transaction amount displayed before completing payment.

JAMB Admission Letter vs Institutional Admission Letter

Newly admitted undergraduates receive two distinct admission documents that serve different administrative purposes.

1. Official JAMB Admission Letter

Issued nationally by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) via e-Facility. It bears your passport photograph, registration number, confirmed institution, degree programme, and a security verification code. It is required for institutional clearance, scholarship vetting, inter-university transfers, and National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) mobilization.

2. Institutional Admission Letter

Issued directly by the Registrar of your admitting university, polytechnic, or college of education via its student portal. It communicates institution-specific onboarding instructions, campus accommodation rules, faculty screening dates, and tuition fee schedules.

Complementary Purpose

Both letters play essential roles during physical clearance. The institutional admission letter guides your campus registration, while the official JAMB admission letter validates your federal matriculation record nationwide.

Step-by-Step: How to Print Your JAMB Admission Letter

Follow these verified steps on the official e-Facility platform to download and print your document.

Step 1

Confirm Your Admission Workflow

Ensure your admission offer has been formally processed and accepted through the JAMB CAPS Portal. Confirming your status ensures your profile reflects the correct institution and programme before printing.

Step 2

Log In to the JAMB e-Facility Portal

Access the official JAMB e-Facility Portal using the registered email address and password tied to your candidate profile.

Step 3

Select "Print Admission Letter"

On the candidate dashboard, open the Admission Services menu and click Print Admission Letter. Select your Examination Year and verify that your JAMB Registration Number displays accurately.

Step 4

Complete Transaction Payment & Download

Review the payment summary generated via Remita (RRR) and complete the transaction online using a debit card or at a designated bank. Once payment clears, the system enables immediate PDF generation for printing.

Official Portal Fees and Accredited Service-Centre Charges

Understand the difference between JAMB's portal fee and third-party printing service charges.

JAMB Official Portal Fee

The statutory portal fee for printing an official JAMB Admission Letter on e-Facility is ₦1,000, subject to confirmation on the Remita/e-Facility transaction checkout page (standard banking or gateway charges may apply).

Centre Charges (2026 Training Manual vs Online Schedule)

If you complete the printing at a centre rather than on a personal device, note that JAMB's 2026 Training Manual lists service charges of ₦200 at JAMB Professional Registration Centres / Professional Test Centres (PRC/PTC) and ₦300 at non-JAMB accredited centres. Because JAMB's live online payment-services schedule may display a different service charge, always confirm the exact amount displayed on the payment screen before authorizing payment.

Print Multiple Copies & Save PDF

Download the original PDF file to a secure personal drive and print at least two clear colored copies. You will typically submit one copy to your faculty/departmental office during clearance and retain the duplicate for personal records.

Procedural Advisory (November 2026)

Verify Current Fees & Portal Schedules

Administrative charges and Remita processing fees referenced from the 2026 registration guidelines are subject to revision by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board. Always verify live service tariffs on the official JAMB e-Facility Portal before finalizing payment.
